How Can Data Systems Become More Fair?

1. Better Data Practices

  • Check whether datasets represent different groups fairly.
  • Identify missing or distorted data.
  • Avoid using harmful proxy variables.

2. Algorithmic Audits

  • Test systems for unequal outcomes.
  • Compare performance across demographic groups.
  • Review both accuracy and fairness.

3. Transparency

  • Explain what signals are being used.
  • Give people information about automated decisions.
  • Make systems understandable to non-experts.

4. Human Oversight

  • Do not rely only on automated decisions.
  • Allow appeals and corrections.
  • Include affected communities in evaluation.

5. Regulation and Policy

  • Create rules for high-risk algorithmic systems.
  • Require accountability when automated systems harm people.
  • Protect privacy and civil rights.
